## Why it wins (mechanism)
A stranger *forbids* a purchase the viewer is making right now → the avatar's own five-year failure list becomes the proof →
the product is a gift, not a pitch (never named, no offer) → the mechanism arrives only after the result, as an answer to
her anger → the ending hands the role of "stranger" to the viewer.

## Visual notes
- Avatar "before" is pushed hard (dark under-eyes, deep lines, flat grey light, unflattering angles); "after" is warm
  window light, styled hair, better wardrobe: the change is sold by lighting and grooming as much as skin.
- Three textures: pharmacy (cinematic, shallow focus), bathroom (iPhone selfie, harsh overhead), kitchen/FaceTime.
- Several shots read as AI (uncanny eyes in the "before" close-ups); the exaggeration is what makes it uncanny.
