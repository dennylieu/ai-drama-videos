(user-named winner), `research/native-swollen-crocs.txt`, `research/9min-cruise-video-copy.txt`. Landing page:

- **Product:** Kolabloom, pure gotu kola (Centella asiatica) capsules. 1,000 mg per two-capsule serving, one
  ingredient, concentrated freeze-dried extract, third-party tested, made in the USA, no fillers.
- **Routine:** two capsules every morning.
- **What it changes (brand's words):** supports healthy circulation in legs, calves and ankles "from the vessel walls
  out"; helps reduce swelling, puffiness and evening sock rings; supports the calf pump ("second heart"); helps the
  body build fresh collagen in vessel walls; legs feel lighter, calves softer.
- **Mechanism in the ads:** "seal, strengthen, restore": swollen legs aren't a fluid problem, the tiny vessel walls
  (and the collagen holding them) leak; water pills drain the surface through the kidneys, compression squeezes from
  outside; gotu kola at a real disclosed dose supports the vessel wall. "Don't just drain it: rebuild the pipes."
- **Proof timeline in the winner:** week 1 nothing ("it isn't a water pill"); week 2 heaviness eases; week 3 sock
  rings shallower; week 4 shoes go on; week 5 ankle bone visible by dinner; week 6 stairs, church, grocery store;
  week 8 shin colour; week 12 doctor's follow-up.
