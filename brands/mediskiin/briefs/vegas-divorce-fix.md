Ad: "Vegas Divorce - early product intro" (8:08, captioned), Mediskiin Test – New Page.

**Diagnosis:** Sale 1 (content) and Sale 2 (hope) are strong but slow to start. Sales 3–4 (value and impulse) are
nearly absent from the film and the copy. The competitor's winner has the opposite profile: Meta rated its engagement
above average and conversion below average, and it carries the impulse sale in the headline ("Buy 1 Get 1 Free Today Only!").

## Fixes (in order of cost)

**A. Cold open, re-cut from existing footage (no regeneration).**
0–8 s: "Exhibit 15. Born June 2nd, 1965." → "She's 61." → "Look at her neck. There's not a single line on it."
→ hard cut to "34! You said 34!" and the film continues. Elements 3, 4 and 5 land in the first 3 seconds.

**B. Hook title test** (first 2 s, burned in): "POV: He married me thinking I was 34" (variants in the copy file).

**C. Close card after "worth it"** (2–4 s, static): offer + guarantee + urgency (elements 15–17) and the anchor
("less than a [day price] a day / no needles, no surgery"). Needs the real offer and guarantee.

**D. Copy and packaging:** offer headline, Brazilified primary text (below), carousel = video + 2 product cards,
reuse the post ID from now on, CTA test Learn More vs Shop Now.

**E. Next generation (later, Seedance):** a 10 s "Conditions" beat (why EGF/NAD+ in drugstore creams doesn't reach
the living layers / Korean formulation) and Denise seen earlier (a 5 s hallway glance at Vivian's neck before the
bathroom scene).
