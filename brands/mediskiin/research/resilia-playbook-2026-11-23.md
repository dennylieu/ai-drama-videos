# RESILIA playbook -> Mediskiin (2026-11-23)

Source: UGC Ninja's "RESILIA Funnel Board" (public breakdown of RESILIA, a supplement brand; Meta Ad Library,
resilia.shop, Trustpilot, Amazon, Google; data collected 2026-11-18). RESILIA is not our competitor; we copy the
**system**, not its ads. Numbers below are the board's, not ours.

## What RESILIA does that works

| # | Pattern (board evidence) | Mediskiin version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Volume from AI video.** 878 new ads in one week, 1,616 live, no shoots. Only 13 ad texts across all of them: the video changes, the copy barely does. | One locked scene body x 3-5 hooks. Each new hook is one 10 s Seedance 480p generation, so a hook costs one generation, not a new film. Target 9 new ads in week 1, then 3-5 replacement hooks a week. |
| 2 | **Cold traffic = family/relationship drama, product late.** Largest group (48 of 128 top creatives) is "unaware": mother-in-law pinning a wedding dress, stadium graduation, cheating husband. 55% of the top 128 are Pixar-style or cinematic AI stories. | Our pain moments already are these scenes (kid's candid photo, mother at Sunday lunch, summer wedding turtleneck). We stay live-action (Harris), not cartoon: one 1-star RESILIA review calls the AI cartoons "vile". |
| 3 | **Every awareness stage gets its own opener.** Unaware: a story. Problem aware: the body ("Bloated but eat clean?"). Solution aware: why this one differs (water test, "20-month aging"). Most aware: the price. | Four buckets below. Mix for week 1 follows RESILIA's top-128 mix, minus the offer bucket until we have an offer. |
| 4 | **The long runners (25-43 days) are not the dramas.** They are: offers on the brand page, "We lied to you / I lied" (challenges what the buyer already bought), a 3D "what actually happens" explainer, and an at-home test ("Stop paying for aged garlic until you do this five-second water test"). Dramas die in a median 7-15 days. | Make at least one "challenge what you already bought" ad and one at-home test ad in week 1: they are the most likely to run long. Plan for drama hooks to fatigue in about 2 weeks. |
| 5 | **Hooks in the buyer's words win, and RESILIA under-uses them.** Its quizzes say "Puffy by dinner?" / "Had to stop on the stairs?"; its ads mostly don't. | Our VOC words go straight into lines and primary text: "turkey neck", "all I can see", "my face looks 35, my neck doesn't", "stick my chin out". |
| 6 | **Copy is the cheap test they skip.** Same 13 texts, one button ("Learn more" on 97%). Competitors all open "Looking to support ... naturally?". | Write 10 primary texts per concept and test them against the same video. |
| 7 | **Persona pages + quiz** ("See your circulation age in 60 seconds", guide character, score, week-by-week plan, then the bundle). | Later, outside this repo: "Find your neck type in 60 seconds" quiz and persona landers (webcam woman, face-neck contrast, surgery-reluctant). Needs the store; not blocking the ads. |
| 8 | **Bundle as the single destination** (2-pack "most popular", gifts unlock with quantity, scratch-card popup). | Offer static ads for the most-aware bucket once price/offer is filled in `context.md`. |

## What we do NOT copy (it is RESILIA's risk, and the board shows it costing them)

- **Fake publisher pages.** 15 of 16 pages are named like health magazines ("Vascular Wellness Report"). Reads as
  editorial, not an ad: Meta policy risk and a trust problem. We run from the brand page (plus real creators later).
- **Claims that don't match the site.** Ads say 4.9 stars / 6,000mg; the site shows 4.5 / 300mg. Every number in our
  ads must match the product page and review site.
- **Hidden subscription.** 65 of 148 negative reviews are surprise charges; Google suggests "Can I cancel my Resilia
  subscription?". If Mediskiin has a refill, say it plainly, then advertise the easy cancel.
- **AI people presented as real.** No AI "customer", "doctor" or "founder" presented as a real person
  (`drama-ad` rule). Story scenes are clearly fiction; no testimonial framing.
- **Surgery comparison.** "Instead of surgery" stays banned (context.md). The surgery consult scene ends with an
  unsigned sheet, no claim.

## Gaps RESILIA leaves that we can take
- Real numbers and real people: if Mediskiin has real reviews, quote them on the end card (verbatim, with the score
  matching the review site).
- Spanish: RESILIA runs 0 Spanish ads; a competitor runs 31. Our lines are short (2-8 words), so a Spanish re-voice of
  a winning scene is one cheap generation. Test after the first English winner.
