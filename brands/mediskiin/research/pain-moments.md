# Mediskiin pain moments (creative-strategy prompt 15)

what the ending can come back to. Q = from a real quote (number = quote # in the VOC doc). I = our idea built on the
research. Scene engine = Harris verdict (someone else observes) or shame (alone with it, partner's flat verdict).
