## Step #1: Premises
1. **Brand name**
2. **Product name**
3. **Ingredients** (and doses)
4. **Attributes** (testing, where made, routine, servings, time to results the brand states, warnings)
5. **Price** (bundles, subscription)
6. **Guarantee**
7. **What do they gain by buying now?**
8. **What do they lose by putting it off?** (only true deadlines / stock)

---

## Step #2: Target
**Question 1: Which markets can our product go after?** List them, pick ONE, say why (the brand's winners and
landers, or competitor traffic growth).

**Question 2: Which segment of that market?** Way 1: copy the segment winners target. Way 2: an unserved one from
Reddit. Say which and why.

**Question 3: Who is the avatar?**
Layer 1: Psychographics: primary desire, problem, secondary desires, pain points, dreams, fears.
Layer 2: Identity: demographics; behaviour (competitor ads, videos, trends, hobbies, news, shows, other conditions);
perspective (beliefs, biases, enemies, allies, language).
