# RK "Don't Buy That": swipe brief (RareKitty Color Shifting Lipstick)

Reference: `brands/rarekitty/refs/winner-1698218391720058/analysis.md` (skincare serum, 5:47, 101 shots). Copy source:
`brands/rarekitty/brazilify.md` (Paint Creep → stain, not paint; wood analogy; truth rules). Offer: `brands/rarekitty/context.md`.

## Why it wins, in one sentence
A stranger forbids the purchase she's making right now, her own years of failed products become the proof, the
product arrives as a gift rather than a pitch, and the mechanism is only explained once she's angry it worked.

## Keep (structure)
- Intervention hook inside 3 s ("Excuse me, ma'am. Don't buy that."), insert of the doomed product in her basket.
- The buyer states the age contrast herself ("How old are you? 50?" "Seventy-one.").
- The failed-solutions inventory with prices, in her bathroom, phone-selfie texture.
- The objection exchange during the handover ("I can't take yours." "I have three at home.").
- A demo moment with a "where did it go?" line, then a short timeline.
- The verdict and the anger ("I'm quite angry with you"), the mechanism explained as an answer.
- Pay-it-forward ending in the same aisle; moral VO over it.
- Product never pitched in dialogue; karaoke captions; no music under dialogue.

## Change (ours)
- Product, category, every line, every face, the store (a drugstore lip aisle, not their pharmacy queue).
- No "we all use it in Korea" or any origin claim (unverified). No collagen-style stats: proof is the on-camera demo.
- Timeline fits a lipstick: minutes, not weeks. Day 1 / Day 3 / Week 2 are about wear (lunch, coffee cup, photos).
- Mechanism in the brief's words: the Paint Creep; it reacts to your lips' pH and warmth; a stain, not paint; the porch
  analogy.

## Acting and casting (owner direction, 2026-11-27)
- **More realistic than the source.** Underplayed, conversational, small reactions. No mugging, no soap-opera beats.
- **The "before" must be very visible but real, not caricature.** Real-looking woman ~60, plain everyday face; the
  problem is on her mouth: red long-wear lipstick feathered into the vertical lines above the lip, dry cracked
  centre, worn-off middle with a ring of liner. Flat store light. No horror make-up, no dead eyes. Uncanny valley is
  the failure mode: plain faces, real skin texture, no airbrushing, no glamour.
- "After" sells by lighting and grooming too: warm window light, hair done, soft rose lips, same face.
- The mentor: a woman ~71 whose lips look naturally rosy and crisp-edged: the visible contrast is age vs lips.

## Fit to Seedance
One location per generation; 15 s = 7–8 lines / 32–40 words; plates for Ruth (avatar), June (mentor), the next woman
and the product (clear stick with gold flakes, gold tube, from the PDP photos). Captions, the porch insert's text, the
end card and offer are built in the edit. Target length ~3:00–3:30 (the reference is 5:47; lipstick needs less
time to prove), about 13–15 segments ≈ $25 at 480p + ~$10 upscale.

## Hook options (same hook type: intervention)
1. ⭐ **"Don't buy that."** Insert: liner and long-wear in her basket. JUNE: "Excuse me, ma'am. Don't buy that." Final
   look: Ruth's lips in close-up, feathered, as she turns. (Closest to the proven hook.)
2. **The mug.** Insert: a coffee cup on the counter with a smeared red print. JUNE (at the next table): "Can I tell you
   something about that cup?" Final look: Ruth touching her mouth.
3. **The photo.** Insert: a family photo on her phone, zoom to her mouth. JUNE (over her shoulder in line): "It's not
   your lips, you know. It's the lipstick." Final look: Ruth, stung.

## Claims check
- Kept: goes on clear, reacts to lip pH and warmth, develops your own rose, a stain not paint, doesn't creep into lip
  lines, not sticky, 90-day money-back guarantee. All from the owner's brief or the live PDP.
- Not used: "1M+ sold", "Developed by Korean Beauty Experts", the Nancy K. review, "3M views", any stat, "natural",
  "clean", "hypoallergenic", "kiss-proof" (no transfer test yet; the mug beat says "nothing on the mug" only if the
  owner's own test supports it).
