## Cast (one locked sentence each, reused in every prompt; plates made first)
- **RUTH, 61 (the buyer).** Plain everyday face, soft jaw, fine vertical lines above the upper lip, shoulder-length
  dark-blonde hair going grey, oatmeal cardigan over a navy top, small silver watch.
  **Before lips:** red long-wear lipstick feathered into the lines above and below the mouth, dry cracked centre, a
  darker liner ring where the colour wore off. Flat, unflattering light. Real, not horror make-up.
  **After lips:** same face, soft rose stain, clean edges, no film; hair brushed, warmer light.
- **JUNE, 71 (the stranger).** Short silver hair, lined face, warm eyes, small gold hoops, camel wool coat, brown
  leather handbag. Lips naturally rosy with crisp edges (the product's rose).
- **DANA, 34 (Ruth's daughter).** Brown ponytail, round glasses, green sweater.
- **TESS and CAROL, 60s (Ruth's friends).** Tess: grey pixie cut, red scarf. Carol: auburn bob, denim shirt.
- **MARGO, 58 (the next woman).** Brunette bob, black puffer jacket, her lips show the same feathered red as Ruth's.
- **The product.** Clear stick with gold flakes, gold metallic tube, clear cap (from the PDP photos). On lips it starts
  clear and glossy, then develops to a soft rose.

Acting: grounded and underplayed, small reactions before each reply, no mugging. Plain faces, real skin texture, no
glamour, no airbrushing (the uncanny-valley rule).

## S1 · Drugstore lip aisle · 10 s · HOOK
1. Insert, high angle: a red plastic basket. A hand drops in a long-wear lipstick, then a lip liner.
2. Medium: RUTH at the shelf. A hand taps her elbow. JUNE, beside her:
   **JUNE:** "Excuse me, ma'am. Don't buy that."
3. **RUTH** turns: "I'm sorry?"
4. **JUNE:** "The liner. Please don't waste your money."
5. Close-up, held: Ruth's mouth as she half-turns, the red feathered into her lines.

## S2 · Same aisle · 15 s · CONTRAST
1. **RUTH**, a little defensive, touching the corner of her mouth: "It's for my lines. Around my mouth."
2. **JUNE**, gently: "I know what it's for."
3. Ruth's eyes drop to June's mouth. Insert: June's lips, rosy and crisp.
4. **RUTH:** "What are you wearing? Your lips look… done."
5. **RUTH:** "How old are you? Fifty-five?"
6. **JUNE:** "Seventy-one."
7. **RUTH**, a beat: "…Seventy-one?"
8. Hold on Ruth.

## S3 · Same aisle · 15 s · FAILED SOLUTIONS
1. **RUTH**, quieter, eyes on the shelf: "Long-wear. Liner. Primer. Blotting."
2. **RUTH:** "By lunch it's in every line."
3. **JUNE:** "And then?"
4. **RUTH:** "Then I gave up. Plain balm for a year."
5. June opens her handbag and takes out a small gold tube.
6. **JUNE:** "Try this one."
7. She uncaps it: the stick is clear with gold flakes. **RUTH:** "It's clear."
8. Hold on Ruth looking at it.

## S4 · Same aisle · 15 s · HANDOVER + OBJECTIONS
1. **JUNE:** "Put it on in your car. Wait three minutes."
2. **RUTH:** "I can't take yours."
3. **JUNE:** "I have two in the car."
4. **RUTH:** "How do I know it won't do the same?"
5. **JUNE:** "Because it isn't paint."
6. June writes a phone number on the back of a receipt from her bag and hands it over. **JUNE:** "Call me. You're going to want to."
7. Ruth puts the lip liner back on the shelf. Hold on her, the gold tube in her hand.

## S5 · Ruth's car, store parking lot · 15 s · DEMO
1. Wide through the windshield: Ruth gets into the driver's seat, a small store bag on the passenger seat.
2. She flips down the visor mirror and wipes the old red off with a tissue. Her bare lips, lined and pale.
3. Close-up: she swipes the clear stick on. Clear and glossy. **RUTH:** "Nothing. It's just balm."
4. She checks her phone, waiting. The parking lot outside.
5. She looks back up at the mirror.
6. Extreme close-up: her lips are now a soft rose, the edges clean.
7. **RUTH**, barely aloud: "Oh. That's… that's mine. That's my pink."
8. Hold on her eyes in the mirror, a little wet.

## S10 · Same call · 15 s · MECHANISM 1 (the Paint Creep)
1. **RUTH:** "Explain it to me. Properly."
2. **JUNE:** "Lipstick is paint. It sits on top."
3. **JUNE:** "Your lips move. Paint doesn't."
4. **JUNE:** "So it cracks, and it creeps into the grooves."
5. **RUTH:** "And the liner?"
6. **JUNE:** "A fence for the paint."
7. Hold on Ruth, nodding slowly.

## S11 · Same call · 15 s · MECHANISM 2 (stain, not paint)
1. **RUTH:** "And yours is different?"
2. **JUNE:** "Mine goes on clear."
3. **JUNE:** "It reacts to your lips. Your warmth, your pH."
4. **JUNE:** "Then it stains. It doesn't paint."
5. **JUNE:** "You wouldn't paint an old porch. You'd stain it."
6. Ruth touches her lips.
7. **RUTH:** "I'm still angry." June laughs quietly. Hold.

  explains the inventory), → kitchen ("DAY 1"), → café ("DAY 3"), → the call (the receipt from S4 on the fridge),
  → dinner ("TWO WEEKS LATER"), → aisle ("A MONTH LATER", the same aisle as S1).
