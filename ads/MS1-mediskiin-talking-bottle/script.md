## Notes before production
- **Claims:** EGF and NAD+ lines use the site's own wording ("signals tired skin to renew itself", "recharges").
  "Firmer-looking", "smoother", "softer" only. No "tightens", "lifts" or "clinically proven" until the brand
  confirms the study. The proof beats are the site's stated timeline plus story moments, not promised results.
- **Real-woman shots are dramatised:** no fake before/afters; if AI-made, they're never presented as a customer.
- **Voice:** one consistent British female voice for the whole film. Best done as a single voiceover track
  (ElevenLabs or Higgsfield voice) laid under the clips, rather than letting each Wan clip invent its own voice;
  bottle clips then get lip-flap animation to the track (Wan 3.0 accepts the audio as a reference).
- **Open:** the brand's list of claims to avoid; confirm the "95% sold out" banner is not used.
