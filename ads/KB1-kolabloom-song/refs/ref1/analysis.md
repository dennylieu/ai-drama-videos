## Measured against the three articles
- **Segment (Carneiro):** not "women with bloating" but **night-shift nurses**. Every detail is segment-specific
  (swollen ankles by hour 6, scrubs, compression socks, the med room, the charge nurse). The close calls them out by
  job: "If you're a nurse...".
- **The problem causes the drama (Zakaria): yes, twice.** The swelling gets her sent home early, so she catches
  them. The night shift, which the mechanism blames for the waste, is the same thing that let the affair happen
  ("she's always on nights"). One cause drives the story and the mechanism. This is the strongest move in the ad.
- **Fear:** betrayal (husband + best friend of 19 years) stacked with rejection ("who's gonna want you like this").
- **Closest-person betrayal, worse, freeze, dismisser, rock bottom (Ryze):** all present, in order.
- **Messenger:** where she'd naturally meet someone with the same problem: the 61-year-old night charge nurse with
  30 years of nights, the finished version of the hero. Credible through her life, not a title.
- **Mechanism (point of contact -> problem -> function -> solution):** "it's hormones / my age / fat" -> it's
  waste, and lymph has no pump -> socks squeeze and diets starve but nothing pumps it, so it backs up ->
  four herbs, one job each. **Conditions:** drops under the tongue skip the stomach. **Visual:** an IV line that
  clogs and everything backs up behind it (a picture every nurse already knows).
- **Proof:** six weeks in the mechanism's order (drain first, then face, ring, shift without socks, size), each one
  noticed by another person. No honest setback week.
- **Payoff flips the hook lines:** "she's always on nights" -> "Relax, I'm on nights"; the stuck ring -> slides off;
  "who's gonna want you" -> paramedics, the doctor's coffee, "are you new?".
- **Close:** job callout + symptom list + "not your age" + product + routine + taste objection + 70 % off + 30-day
  guarantee + the final callback.

## Differences from our skill as written
1. **Cold open:** it starts on the payoff (the slap), then "Let me tell you how I got there." The loop is "what
   was the one sentence?", closed at 6:06. Add as an option.
2. **No classic chorus.** Instead, a **motif line** repeats with a new meaning each time ("she's always on nights"
   x4, "out cold before she hit the floor" x2), and the mechanism is restated once at the end. Either a chorus or a
   motif can carry the mechanism.
3. **197 words a minute**, not 130-150. Plan length from this.
4. **Animated, not live action.** Our pipeline is live-action Seedance; it can also make stylised 3D animation, a
   style decision for the user. The mechanism shots are 3D medical animation either way.
5. **What we don't copy:** the nudity on the couch, the violence played for cheers, the brand's look, lines,
   characters or the "toxins/waste" claims (Kolabloom needs its own true mechanism).
