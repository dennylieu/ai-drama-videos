## Implication
Our two bathroom beats (demo, then "what's it cost / what if it doesn't work") are exactly the winner's blocks. But
the bigger lesson is **structure**: the winner is a single sales conversation wrapped in a 5-second hook. Our film is a
crime story with a sales scene inside it.
