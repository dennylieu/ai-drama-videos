Same light as the existing bathroom: flat fluorescent overhead, grey stalls, marble sinks.

**N1: Conditions + demo (15 s, 7 lines, about 22 words)**

3. Denise, a small nod: "…Yes."
4. Vivian: "Then it sat on top. Give me your hand."
5. Insert: two red drops on the back of Denise's hand; Vivian presses and rubs them in with two fingers; the red fades into the skin as it is rubbed.
6. Denise stares at her hand: "Where did it go?"

**N2: The problem made vivid + skeptic's testimony + timeline (15 s, about 34 words)**
